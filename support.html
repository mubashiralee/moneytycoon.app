Common topics

  • Purchases and subscriptions: Mogul Premium, gem packs, Remove Ads, and other in-app purchases are delivered through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Use Restore Purchases in Settings if entitlements do not appear after a verified purchase.
  • Ads: Free players may see optional rewarded ads and passive banner ads on some screens. Remove Ads and qualifying subscriptions suppress advertisements. Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Backup and restore: On Android, Google Play Games may back up progress when signed in. On iOS, optional iCloud backup uses CloudKit. Sign in with Apple links Mogul Exchange online identity and is separate from iCloud backup. Restore coverage depends on the systems you use and the current app version.
  • Online features: Mogul Exchange and other online modes require network access and may be limited by server maintenance or device verification.
