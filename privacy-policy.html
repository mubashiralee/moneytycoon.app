Privacy Policy for Money Tycoon

Developer and data controller: CapitalCraft Studios

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Plain-language summary
  • Money Tycoon is available on Android (Google Play) and iOS (Apple App Store). This policy covers both platforms. Platform-specific details are called out below.
  • On first launch, the App creates an anonymous Firebase account so online features can work without requiring an email address.
  • Most game processing happens on your device. Optional cloud backup, notifications, purchases, and rewarded ads are under your control on each platform.
  • Android: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect diagnostics where consent law requires opt-in. Google Play Games Saved Games may back up progress when signed in. Linking a Google account is Mogul Exchange / online identity, not empire backup.
  • iOS: First-party Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled in current releases. Ads use Google AdMob with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Empire backup on iOS uses CloudKit / iCloud. Optional Sign in with Apple or Link Google Account (when OAuth is configured) is Mogul Exchange / online identity, not iCloud backup. Google Play Games Saved Games are not available on iOS.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data in Settings or by email.

1. Scope

This policy explains how CapitalCraft Studios ("we", "our", or "us") processes information when you use the Money Tycoon mobile application ("Money Tycoon" or the "App") on Android or iOS. Money Tycoon is a simulation game. Balances, holdings, trades, and other financial values inside the game are virtual game data, not real bank or brokerage accounts.

2. Information we process

Local game data

Android stores game progress on your device using Room, DataStore, and protected preferences.

iOS stores game progress on your device using Core Data, UserDefaults, and protected preferences.

On both platforms this includes your player name, selected country and persona, virtual balances, businesses, properties, investments, orders, achievements, settings, tutorial state, and purchase entitlement state.

Anonymous account and optional linked identity

On first launch, the App automatically asks Firebase Authentication to create a random user ID. No email address is required for this anonymous account.

Android: You may optionally sign in to Google Play Games for Saved Games restore, and optionally link a Google account for Mogul Exchange / online identity. Linking Google is not Android empire backup. If you link Google, Firebase may receive the email address, provider user ID, display name, and authentication token supplied by Google. We do not receive your Google password.

iOS: You may optionally use Sign in with Apple to link your Firebase account for Mogul Exchange / online identity. That is not iCloud backup. When Google OAuth is configured on the build, Settings may also offer Link Google Account for the same Exchange / online identity path. Apple or Google may provide an email and provider user ID. We do not receive passwords. Google Play Games Saved Games are not available on iOS.

Cloud save and game-service data

Android: Restore authority is Google Play Games Saved Games when you are signed in. Linking a Google account (Firebase Auth) is Mogul Exchange / online identity, not empire backup. Firestore gameplay snapshots are not the Android restore path and remain disabled by default. Play Games Saved Games snapshots may be written when Play Games sign-in is active.

iOS: Empire backup and restore use CloudKit / iCloud. Sign in with Apple (and optional Link Google when OAuth is configured) is Mogul Exchange / online identity, not iCloud backup. iOS does not use Google Play Games. Restore coverage on iOS is limited to the systems implemented in the current release; the App discloses restore limitations in Settings when applicable.

Play Games Saved Games snapshots (Android) and CloudKit / iCloud copies (iOS) hold empire restore data when those backup paths are used. Firebase may still hold identity, notification tokens, and Mogul Exchange / online records keyed to your Firebase user ID. Firestore gameplay snapshots are not the Android restore path.

Purchases

Android: Google Play processes purchases. We receive purchase tokens, product identifiers, transaction status, and entitlement information needed to verify and deliver a purchase.

iOS: Apple App Store processes purchases through StoreKit. We receive transaction identifiers, product identifiers, and entitlement information needed to verify and deliver a purchase.

We do not receive full payment-card or bank-account details on either platform.

Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service (iOS) may issue a per-installation token. When registered, the App may store the token with your Firebase user ID, locale, selected country, notification preferences, app version, device model, and OS version. In-app notification settings and OS permission controls determine whether notifications are shown. Server-side frequency controls limit engagement messages.

Google Play Games (Android only)

On Android, Google Play Games sign-in is optional. If used, the App may receive a Player ID and display name for authentication, achievements, Saved Games, and global leaderboards. The App may submit game statistics such as virtual net worth, business/property counts, passive income, prestige, achievements, trade profit, and virtual tax paid. These leaderboard scores are reported by the game client and are not verified from a server-owned gameplay ledger; the App labels that limitation on the leaderboard screen.

iOS: Google Play Games is not available on iOS. Empire leaderboards on iOS use on-device records and, when online features are enabled, Firestore-backed boards.

Advertising and consent

On both platforms, the App may show passive inline banner ads to free players on selected informational screens. It also offers rewarded ads and rewarded-interstitial bonus offers that play only after you deliberately choose to watch.

Google AdMob and enabled mediation partners may process an advertising or device identifier, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and diagnostic information about ad performance for ad delivery, measurement, frequency control, fraud prevention, and compliance. Depending on region and consent, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Android: The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) stores and communicates your advertising consent choices. You can reopen privacy choices from Settings and reset or delete the Android advertising ID in system settings.

iOS: UMP presents advertising consent where required. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt controls access to the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) for cross-app tracking. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and reset the advertising identifier under Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. The App's privacy manifest declares tracking and the AdMob tracking domain googleads.g.doubleclick.net.

Remove Ads and qualifying ad-free subscriptions suppress all ad formats. A banner-only pass suppresses passive banners for its purchased duration but does not suppress rewarded offers or other ad formats.

Potential mediation partners include Meta Audience Network, Pangle, Unity, Liftoff/Vungle, AppLovin, ironSource, Mintegral, InMobi, and Digital Turbine/Fyber. A partner receives an ad request only when selected by the AdMob mediation configuration and permitted by applicable consent signals.

Diagnostics, analytics, and configuration

Android: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, device information, and an anonymous user ID where privacy law requires consent (for example the EEA and UK), and may be enabled by default elsewhere. You can revisit consent choices from Settings.

iOS: First-party Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled in current App Store builds (FIREBASE_ANALYTICS_COLLECTION_ENABLED=false). Third-party ad SDKs may still process limited diagnostic data when an ad is requested. Firebase Remote Config and Firebase App Check (including Apple App Attest where enabled) may process installation and integrity signals needed to deliver configuration and protect online features securely.

Read-only market-data providers

Optional Pro Trading screens may request public market prices from providers such as Alpha Vantage, Finnhub, or Alpaca. Requests use HTTPS and may expose ordinary connection information such as IP address and user agent to the provider.

User-directed sharing

Android: The Tycoon Card feature generates an image on your device. Money Tycoon does not upload the image. If you choose Share or Save, Android sends it to the destination you select.

iOS: The same feature uses the system share sheet. Money Tycoon does not upload the image; the destination you choose processes it under its own terms.

Chat, Messages and Profile Photos

If you use Mogul Exchange chat, we process the messages you send (floor, direct messages and syndicates), reactions, your handle and display name, and any profile photo you upload. Photos are resized on your device before upload. This data is "Other User Content" and "Photos or Videos" in Apple's App Privacy terms, is linked to your account, and is used only to run the chat and to moderate it (automatic screening, reports and staff review). It is stored on our game server exchange.moneytycoon.duckdns.org, is visible to other players in the rooms you post in, and is deleted when you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account).

Birth year. Before your first chat post we ask, once, for the year you were born. We store only the year, linked to your account on the same server, to decide how chat works for you; it is not shown to other players and cannot be changed afterwards. If the answer means you may be under 13, chat becomes read-only (the public Floor only, no direct messages, syndicates or profile photo), any profile photo is removed, and ads on that device are requested with Google's child-directed treatment and without the App Tracking Transparency prompt. The answer is kept if you start a new game and is deleted when you delete your account. This is "Other Data Types" in Apple's App Privacy terms.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use Google Firebase, Google AdMob, mediation partners listed above, Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple, iCloud / CloudKit, push notifications, App Attest), and on Android Google Play and Google Play Games. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising identifiers and related data may be "shared" for cross-context behavioural advertising under some privacy laws when personalized advertising is permitted.

  • Game services operated by us — exchange.moneytycoon.duckdns.org runs Mogul Exchange, the Auction House, the Trading Floor and chat. It receives your account ID, sign-in token, trades and listings, and chat content.
  • Discord (optional) — if you link Discord to claim a welcome reward, we store the link between your game account and your Discord user ID and a referral code. Discord processes your Discord account under its own privacy policy.

Provider policies include Google's Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, AdMob privacy information, and Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Retention

Local data remains until you reset it, clear app storage, delete the account, or uninstall. Cloud game data remains while the account is active or until deletion succeeds. Purchase records and entitlement evidence may be retained where required to prevent duplicate fulfillment, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Apple or Google may retain store purchase history under their policies even after Money Tycoon account deletion.

7. Data deletion and account deletion

Delete in the App

  1. Open Money Tycoon.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm.

This process requests deletion of the Firebase account, Firestore game data, server-authoritative exchange/rebirth records, registered notification data, and Money Tycoon-managed cloud saves, then clears local game data.

Android: If a recent Google reauthentication is required, the App will ask you to sign in again before continuing. Play Games snapshots may require separate cleanup through Google account controls.

iOS: Sign in with Apple reauthentication may be required before deletion completes. Google Play Games steps do not apply on iOS.

Request deletion without the App

Email capitalcraftgames@gmail.com with the subject Money Tycoon account deletion request. Include the linked Apple or Google email or any player/account identifier you still have. Do not send a password or payment-card information. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

What may remain

App Store or Google Play purchase history, active/cancelled subscription records, platform achievements, fraud-prevention records, and records legally required for accounting or dispute handling may remain under applicable retention rules.

Reset Game starts a new playthrough and is not the same as Delete Account. Clear storage or uninstall removes local data but does not reliably delete cloud data.

8. Your choices and rights

Both platforms: Manage ad privacy choices and notification categories in Settings; request access, correction, export, restriction, objection, or deletion where applicable.

Android: Decline optional Google/Play Games linking; use Android advertising controls.

iOS: Decline ATT tracking; manage Sign in with Apple from Settings; use iOS advertising controls described above.

EEA/UK users may withdraw consent and complain to their local supervisory authority. California residents may request to know, correct, or delete covered information and may opt out of sharing used for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not discriminate for exercising privacy rights.

9. Children

Money Tycoon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Chat asks for a birth year before a player's first post; a player whose answer means they may be under 13 can only read the public Floor, cannot post, send direct messages, join syndicates or upload a photo, and is served child-directed ads without the tracking prompt (see Chat, Messages and Profile Photos above). If you believe a child provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.
