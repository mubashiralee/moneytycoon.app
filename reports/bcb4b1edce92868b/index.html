    Basis of preparation, data quality and definitions

    Where the numbers come from

      Data-quality flags

      Definitions

      • Advertising revenue — AdMob estimated earnings, already net of the ad network's share. It is the amount payable to the publisher.
      • Gross billings — what players paid for in-app purchases, before the store's commission. The source column is labelled "includes fees".
      • Net revenue — advertising revenue plus in-app purchases after store commission and any other deductions. This is the top line of the statement.
      • eCPM — advertising revenue per thousand ad impressions.
      • ARPDAU — net revenue divided by daily active users, averaged only over days where the user metric is reported.
      • Operating profit — net revenue less cost of revenue and operating expenses; before tax. Equivalent to EBITDA for a business with no debt or depreciation.

      Method and limitations

      • All arithmetic is performed in integer pence; nothing is stored as a floating-point pound value, so column and row totals reconcile exactly.
      • Recurring expenses are amortised evenly across the days of each period. The remainder from division is distributed penny-by-penny, so the days of a month always sum to the exact monthly amount.
      • Tax is applied to each displayed period's operating profit where positive; the total column is the sum of the periods shown.
      • Refunds appear as negative purchase earnings. Store commission is reversed on them at the same rate.
      • This is an indicative management report built from platform reporting, not a statutory account. It is not tax or accounting advice, and platform figures are themselves estimates until paid.
