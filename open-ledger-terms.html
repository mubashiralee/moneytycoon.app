The Open Ledger, Edition 1: Full Terms

Rules version 1.1. Entries open 13 October 2026 13:00 UTC.

No purchase necessary. Free to enter. Not sponsored by or associated with Apple, Google or Discord. Void where prohibited. Promoter: the Money Tycoon Team. Contact: capitalcraftgames@gmail.com. English rules govern.

1. What

Post one screenshot of your own Money Tycoon game in the #open-ledger channel of the Money Tycoon Discord server with a one-line caption starting #empire, #comeback or #moment. Any screen counts. Full screen, uncropped, Privacy Mode off, no edits.

  • One screenshot per Discord account. To change it, delete and repost before entries close.
  • The caption is required, 50 words or fewer, and starts with the category tag.
  • Android and iOS players are both eligible.

2. When

  • Entries open 13 October 2026 13:00 UTC and close 22 October 2026 23:59 UTC.
  • Voting runs 23 to 25 October 2026 (until 25 October 23:59 UTC).
  • Winners are announced 27 October 2026.
  • If fewer than 10 valid entries arrive by 20 October, entries may be extended once by 4 days, and we will say so. The contest is never cancelled after people have entered.

4. To be paid

Link your game account to Discord before 27 October: type !mt code in Discord, Emma DMs you a code, redeem it in the app under Settings → Redeem Discord Code. Linking also pays the usual 165 welcome gems if you have not had them. Prize gems arrive the next time you open the game. Payout is made to the linked game account, never to a typed handle or ID.

5. Judging (Grand Empire and Comeback Ledger)

The studio lead judges, using the published rubric below, with an AI first pass (Claude Opus 5.5, claude-opus-5-5) that sees no names: only the image, the caption and the category tag. All scores are published with the winners, together with the prompt, the model id and every change the judge made with its reason. Ties on total go to the higher caption score, then the earlier entry.

6. Community Choice vote

  • Staff add a single 🏆 reaction to every entry that passes the entry rules; that reaction also marks the entry as valid. Members vote by tapping 🏆.
  • Votes count only from members who joined the server before voting opened and have passed the welcome gate. Votes from Discord accounts under 30 days old are discarded.
  • A member may vote for any number of entries, once each. Entrants may vote, including for themselves.
  • A burst of 20 or more new 🏆 reactions within an hour, mostly from accounts that joined that day, is reviewed by staff, and those votes are discarded if they do not pass the rules above.
  • The count that matters is the staff export at close, not the number on screen. The final tally is posted with the winners.

7. Open Draw

Every valid entry is numbered in posting order. The seed is the Discord message ID of the "entries are closed" post in #open-ledger, published before the draw. A short published script picks three entries from that seed; the command and its output are posted, and anyone can rerun it.

8. Eligibility and verification

  • One entry per person. Discord account at least 30 days old at entry close. Age 18+.
  • Staff, moderators, persona accounts and accounts under enforcement cannot enter.
  • Entries that break server rules are removed. The same device or screen appearing in entries from different accounts removes both.
  • Before payout, the top three Grand Empire entries and both Comeback Ledger finalists are asked for a 10 to 20 second screen recording of the same screen as their entry (scrolling or tapping once), by DM to Emma, within 48 hours. Without one, the prize passes to the next in line.

9. Publicity

By entering you let us repost your screenshot with your Discord name and name you as a winner.

10. Objections

Objections within 3 working days of the winners post, by DM to Emma. They are answered by the studio lead.
